# Cockro living combat review

Experimental branch, not an Android release. The original praying mascot is no longer the combat renderer. Current review uses ruby/gold armored Cockro poses for ready, sword anticipation, strike, recovery, casting, hurt and defeat. Animation remains discrete key poses, not production-quality skeletal animation. Walking artwork FAILED visual acceptance because opposite stride phases remain too similar. Do not publish this branch as a finished character/game or claim MU-equivalent quality.

The video is an isolated animation review rendered using the game's drawHero function. The background is a neutral review stage, not a completed game map. It is not a multiplayer or physical Android recording. Gameplay tests separately verify damage timing, cancellation, range and casting.

Implementation: melee damage resolves at 50% of the swing duration, once only. Attack and spell windups block conflicting actions. Hurt cancels pending impact; death prevents release. The target must still be alive, present and in reach. Haste scales impact and pose timelines together. Spell effects resolve at casting release. Hits produce short recoil, brightness flash and sparks. Pausing freezes actions; changing maps clears pending action.

Validation: 30 automated tests and syntax check pass. Inspected ready/anticipation/strike/cast/hurt/death render frames. Source rectangles are registered per combat pose to avoid neighboring atlas cells. Walking, direction coverage and transition continuity still block release; full monster animation and real-device frame-rate testing are outstanding. Google login and multiplayer remain unconnected in the 2.5D client.

Art generated with built-in image generation, using the original COCKRO mascot as identity reference. Selected source is the first 4x4 atlas; later walking and 3x2 variants were rejected. Converted to WebP for packaging, alpha retained. The renderer uses pose-specific crop and foot registration; no segmented body rig is used.

## Selected asset prompt

Reference is identity only: heroic red cockroach with antennae and cyan accents. Redesign as a magnificent original dark fantasy MMORPG armored insect knight: ruby metallic segmented armor, aged gold trim, cyan energy joints, compact dark teal cape, one steel sword. Mature heroic proportions and detailed polished game render, high readability at 120px. NOT praying, NOT palms together. Same character, same armor, same camera/scale in all 16 cells. Whole body and weapon fully contained within each cell, 12px margin. Feet grounded at same baseline y=235 of every cell; center x=128, character body roughly170px tall with antennae. Orthographic isometric 3/4 view looking southeast except second row which is rear northeast. Exact poses by row: ROW1 four-frame FRONT walking loop: left foot forward right back; feet passing with right knee lifted; right foot forward left back; feet passing with left knee lifted. Opposite arms, sword held safely at side; visibly different leg positions in each frame. ROW2 same four-frame WALK seen from REAR. ROW3 four-frame FRONT sword strike: anticipation sword pulled behind shoulder and weight on back leg; acceleration twisting body sword overhead; IMPACT broad forward diagonal sword slash with front knee bent; follow-through blade low on opposite side returning to ready. Clearly different silhouette each frame, no duplicated pose. ROW4: combat-ready idle with sword down; casting with free palm extended cyan energy; hurt recoil with chest pulled backward; DEFEATED lying flat on ground. No floor or background or text, true transparent alpha. No connecting trails across cells. Each pose an independent complete sprite. Maintain identity and registration for animation.
